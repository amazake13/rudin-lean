Let \(K \in L^1(\mathbb R^n)\) have nowhere-zero Fourier transform. If \(f \in L^\infty \) satisfies \((K \star f)(x) \to A \int K\), then \((h \star f)(x) \to A \int h\) for every \(h \in L^1\).
